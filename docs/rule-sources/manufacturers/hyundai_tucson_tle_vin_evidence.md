# Hyundai Tucson TLe supporting evidence

Accessed 2026-09-15.

This note records applicable first-party and government-hosted evidence for the
Czech/European Tucson represented by the supplied `TMAJ381A...` VIN11 values.
It does not claim that either source publishes a VIN character table.

## Czech Ministry of Transport / Hyundai Motor Czech

Source: statement 032/23, dated 21 June 2023, hosted by the Czech Ministry of
Transport and signed by the technical department of Hyundai Motor Czech:

https://md.gov.cz/getattachment/Dokumenty/Silnicni-doprava/SME/Stanoviska-vyrobcu/mereni-emisi-u-motoru-D4HA_REVIZE-21-6-2023.pdf.aspx?lang=cs-CZ

The statement describes Hyundai Tucson type `TLe` and identifies `D4HA` as an
engine type used in the covered Hyundai ix35/Tucson vehicles. Its subject and
opening text identify Hyundai Motor Czech as the accredited representative.

This grounds the TLe/D4HA combination. It does not state that VIN position 8
`A` means D4HA, and the stated 100 kW D4HA case is not extrapolated to the
136 kW NZ vehicles.

## Hyundai Motor Company regional specification

Source: Hyundai Asia & Pacific, TUCSON 2018 performance page:

https://www.hyundai.com/pacific/en/find-a-car/tucson-2018/performance

- The page lists a 2.0 CRDi diesel engine at 185 PS and 4,000 rpm.
- The same model page lists an 8-speed automatic transmission.
- Hyundai warns that specifications can differ by region, so these claims only
  corroborate the powertrain family observed in the NZ rows.

## OEM workshop-manual mirror already used by the profile

Source: Hyundai Tucson TL 2016 workshop-manual preview:

https://www.emanualonline.com/cars/hyundai/tucson/2016-hyundai-tucson-tl-g-1-6-t-gdi-service-repair-manual-emo-583485

The preview supplies the position layout currently represented by the profile.
It is a petrol TL manual; applying its character roles and code meanings to a
Czech-built TLe diesel remains explicitly inferred.

## Deliberate limits

- VIN position 8 `A` remains unresolved because none of the accepted sources
  links that character to D4HA or 2.0 CRDi.
- The `TMA`, `J`, `3`, `8`, `1`, `S`, year-code and plant-code readings remain
  inferred where the only character table is the petrol-manual mirror.
- No decoded fact names or auto-accepts a TecDoc kType.
